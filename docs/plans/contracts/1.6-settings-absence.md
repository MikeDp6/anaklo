## 0. Decisions (defaults binding for the builders; approved by Michalis on 2026-10-02)

**Database**
1. ⚠ **`reassign_candidates` RPC instead of filtering `staff_available_slots`** (the plan names the latter). `available_slots_core` only returns starts on the `slot_step_min` grid, but appointments can be off the grid (1.4 gap presets such as 10:40, or a later change of the step), so the slot list would say «nobody is free» when a colleague is. The new RPC asks exactly what `move_core` will check (`booking_staff_terms` + `staff_time_check` with the colleague's terms). The move itself is still `staff_move_appointment` with notify, as planned.
2. **Two more RPCs for atomic, idempotent writes:** `save_service` (service row **and** its complete staff list with custom duration/price in one call; with direct writes a lost answer between the two statements leaves half a service) and `set_staff_order` (one call per reorder). Weekly hours only through `replace_week_hours` (plan). Closures, time off and staff rows stay direct table writes (plan), with client-generated ids (D17).
3. **Grants tightened in 0008** (§2.3): no direct writes at all on `services`, `staff_services`, `service_categories`, `working_hours` (RPC or provisioning only; their `_write` policies are dropped); `staff`, `schedule_exceptions`, `time_off` get **column** INSERT/UPDATE grants instead of table-wide ones; no DELETE on `staff` (soft delete via `active`, SPEC §7); no UPDATE on `schedule_exceptions` (closures are added and deleted, never edited); `businesses.theme` loses its UPDATE grant (the theme changes only by script, plan §1.6). Staff could not write any of these before either (RLS); 0008 closes the owner's unused paths.
4. **`schedule_exceptions.note` only on shop-wide rows** (new CHECK `schedule_exceptions_note_shop_only`): every member reads every exception, so a free-text note on a staff member's day off could carry health data to colleagues (GDPR art. 9). The UI offers the note only for the whole shop («Αργία»).
5. **Existing overlapping `time_off` rows are not merged:** `alter table … add constraint time_off_no_overlap` fails loudly if any exist. None exist (seed and pgTAP fixtures have none; provisioning never writes `time_off`; the remote has no app data). Fix by hand if it ever fails.
6. **Re-plan of queued reminders (closes 1.5 D8):** a change of `quiet_start`, `quiet_end` or `reminder_mode` re-plans the business's queued, not-yet-due, never-attempted reminders with the same `private.reminder_at` (planning instant = the row's `created_at`); a reminder whose new time is null or already past is cancelled `superseded` (never sent late, D3 of 1.5). Without it, a new quiet window silently expires reminders at claim (1.5 claim rule 7(d)). Trigger on `businesses`, so provisioning gets it too. SPEC §12 «Όπως υλοποιήθηκε στο 1.5» line about settings changes is updated.
7. **`schedule_conflicts`** and every 1.6 RPC are **owner/manager only** (staff → `42501`), except `reassign_candidates` (owner/manager, or the appointment's own staff member, as `staff_move_appointment`). Conflicts compare the **service time** `[starts_at, ends_at)`, not the buffer (a buffer past closing time inconveniences nobody). Default range: from `now()`, horizon **366 days**; longer → AN002. The rows carry the client's name and phone (owner/manager may read them anyway) and **reason codes only**, never `time_off.reason`.
8. **`mark_absence`**: owner/manager only; writes `reason 'leave'`; an existing time off that covers the window is kept as is (its reason too); one that partly overlaps is **extended** (several are merged into the earliest); returns the conflicts of the requested window only. Window from the UI: **from now until the next local midnight** (SPEC flow 6 «άδεια για σήμερα»); longer absences go through «Άδειες».
9. **No staff_day_windows duplication:** 0008 extracts the precedence rule (staff exception > shop exception > weekly hours) into `private.staff_day_opening` and replaces the **body** of `private.staff_day_windows` (0004, signature unchanged) with «opening − time off»; `08_availability` must pass unchanged.

**Screens**
10. **Absence flow defaults:** reassignment at the same time with «Ενημέρωση με SMS» **off** by default (the time does not change; SMS cost); cancellation with SMS **on** by default, reason `staff_unavailable` (`shop_closed` when the conflict says the shop is closed), no reason picker; no D8 squeeze (AN005/AN006 never offered here). No colleague free → only «Ακύρωση με SMS» or «Άνοιγμα στο ημερολόγιο» (the 1.4 day view, where `MoveFlow` moves it to another time); the row stays unresolved.
11. **Closures:** add and delete only (to change one, delete and add); a range of up to **62 dates** for both kinds; «έξτρα ώρες» is labelled **«Ειδικό ωράριο»** because an `open` exception **replaces** that day's weekly hours (0004 precedence; a shop-wide one applies to every staff member without their own exception, even on their day off); its intervals are prefilled with the chosen staff member's weekly hours of the first date (shop: 09:00–17:00).
12. **«Αντιγραφή σε όλες τις μέρες»** copies the day's intervals to all seven days, closed ones included (literal reading of the plan); nothing is saved until «Αποθήκευση».
13. **Services:** a category picker lists the provisioned categories (and «Χωρίς κατηγορία»); categories themselves are not created/renamed in the app (plan). No service reordering in 1.6; a new service goes last. Deactivate instead of delete.
14. **Staff:** name, colour (a palette of 8 + the current colour if it is not in it), active, order (▲/▼ buttons, no drag); no photo; staff without login are created here, logins/invitations stay 1.7. Deactivating someone with future appointments is allowed; they show up in «Ραντεβού που χρειάζονται αλλαγή» with reason `staff_inactive`.
15. **No business profile screen in 1.6:** name, phone, locale, address, maps link and theme stay script-only (provisioning); the provisioning JSON gains `address`, `maps_url`, `quiet_start`, `quiet_end`, `reminder_mode` (§2.9). `BookingPolicyForm` also carries `messaging_enabled` («Υπενθυμίσεις με SMS»), which the provisioning JSON has.
16. **Who sees what:** owner and manager see every 1.6 screen (SPEC §5: the manager is the owner without billing/critical actions); staff see only «Ειδοποιήσεις» and get redirected from the other routes. No staff self-service (own hours, own time off) in 1.6.
17. **Idempotent writes everywhere** (an unknown outcome locks the form and offers the identical retry, 1.4 §0.15): new rows carry a **client-generated uuid** and are sent as `upsert(…, { onConflict: 'id', ignoreDuplicates: true })` (`INSERT … ON CONFLICT (id) DO NOTHING`), so a retry of a write that did commit inserts nothing and succeeds; updates, deletes, `save_service`, `set_staff_order`, `replace_week_hours`, `mark_absence` are idempotent by content.
18. **Minute values in `BookingPolicyForm`** are preset selects (§4.8); a stored value that is not a preset (from provisioning) is shown as an extra option and saved back unchanged.
19. **e2e shops:** settings and absence e2e run on dedicated synthetic shops that the spec provisions locally (one per browser project), never on `demo-barber` (other specs book there). The absence spec needs ≥ 2 h left in the shop's local day (two future appointments today): its shop is in Europe/Athens while ≥ 3 h are left there and in America/New_York (`e2e-absence-ny-<project>`) otherwise, so it never skips (changed at the final check, 2026-10-02: a skip from ~21:40 Athens time hid flow 6 from evening CI runs).
20. **Estimate:** the extra RPCs, the refactor and the re-plan add ≈ 0.5 day to the 4 days of 1.6 (taken from the buffer if needed).

### 2.4 Helpers (private, **no grants**)

- `private.staff_day_opening(p_business_id uuid, p_staff_id uuid, p_local_date date) returns table (scope text, closed boolean, windows tstzmultirange)` (stable) — **exactly one row**: `scope` = the winning scope (`'staff'` if the staff member has any exception that date, else `'shop'` if the shop has any, else `'weekly'`); `closed` = the winning scope has a `closed` row; `windows` = its `open` intervals (or the weekly hours) as instants via `(date + time) at time zone businesses.timezone`, merged; empty multirange when closed or no hours. This is the 0004 precedence text moved, unchanged.
- `private.staff_day_windows` (0004): **body replaced** (`create or replace`, same signature, same rows): `unnest(opening.windows − coalesce(time_off multirange, '{}'))`, ordered by start. `08_availability` is its regression test.
- `private.schedule_conflicts_core(p_business_id uuid, p_staff_id uuid, p_from timestamptz, p_to timestamptz)` returns the row type of §2.5.2 (stable). Candidates: appointments of the business, `status in ('booked','confirmed')`, `p_staff_id is null or staff_id = p_staff_id`, `starts_at < p_to and ends_at > p_from`. For each, `S = [starts_at, ends_at)`, `D` = the local dates S touches; `reasons` (in this order, duplicates removed):
  1. `shop_closed` / `staff_closed`: some d ∈ D has `opening.closed` with scope `shop` / `staff`;
  2. `time_off`: a `time_off` row of the staff member overlaps S;
  3. `special_hours` / `outside_hours`: no d ∈ D is closed and S is not inside the union of `opening.windows` over D — `special_hours` when some d ∈ D has scope `staff`/`shop`, else `outside_hours`;
  4. `staff_inactive`: `staff.active` is false.
  A row is returned iff `reasons` is not empty (equivalently: S not inside the union of `staff_day_windows` over D, or the staff member is inactive). Order: `starts_at`, staff `sort`, `staff_id`, `appointment_id`.
- `private.replan_reminders_impl(p_business_id uuid, p_now timestamptz default now()) returns integer` and trigger function `private.businesses_replan_reminders()`: §2.6.

#### 2.5.1 `replace_week_hours`

1. Membership; `p_staff_id` of the business (`42501`, also when null); role.
2. Shape (`22023`, nothing changed): `p_rows` a JSON array of ≤ 70 objects, each with **exactly** the keys `weekday` (integer 0–6, 0 = Sunday), `start_time`, `end_time` (strings `HH:MM`; start `00:00`–`23:59`, end `00:01`–`24:00`), `end_time > start_time`. `[]` is valid (no hours at all).
3. `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended('week_hours:' || p_business_id || ':' || p_staff_id, 0))` (two editors: the last one wins, never a mix).
4. Same set as stored (weekday, start, end) → no write, `changed: false`. Else `delete` all rows of the staff member, then **one** `insert … select` of the new rows. Overlapping rows → the exclusion `working_hours_no_overlap` raises **`23P01`**, which propagates unchanged; the delete rolls back with it (the call is one transaction): **nothing changes**.
5. **Booked appointments outside the new hours: nothing happens to them.** They are reported, not touched.

Returns `{ staff_id, changed, rows: [{ weekday, start_time: 'HH:MM', end_time: 'HH:MM' }] (by weekday, start_time), conflict_count }`, `conflict_count` = `count(*)` of `schedule_conflicts_core(p_business_id, p_staff_id, now(), now() + interval '366 days')`.

#### 2.5.2 `schedule_conflicts`

1. Membership; `p_staff_id` null (= every staff member) or of the business (`42501`); role.
2. `v_from = coalesce(p_from, now())`, `v_to = coalesce(p_to, v_from + interval '366 days')`; `v_to <= v_from` → `22023`; `v_to - v_from > interval '366 days'` → **AN002**.
3. `return query select * from schedule_conflicts_core(p_business_id, p_staff_id, v_from, v_to)`.

Rows (exactly these columns): `appointment_id uuid, staff_id uuid, starts_at timestamptz, ends_at timestamptz, status text, source text, client_id uuid, client_name text, client_phone_e164 text, service_ids uuid[], reasons text[]`. `client_name`/`client_phone_e164` null for a walk-in without client or an erased client; `service_ids` by position; `reasons` ⊆ `CONFLICT_REASONS` = `['shop_closed','staff_closed','time_off','special_hours','outside_hours','staff_inactive']`. **No column ever carries `time_off.reason` or an exception's note.**

#### 2.5.3 `mark_absence`

1. Membership; `p_staff_id` of the business (`42501`); role; `p_from`/`p_to` null or `p_to <= p_from` or `p_to - p_from > interval '31 days'` → `22023`; staff member inactive → **AN008**.
2. `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended('time_off:' || p_business_id || ':' || p_staff_id, 0))`.
3. `O` = the staff member's `time_off` rows with `tstzrange(starts_at, ends_at) && tstzrange(p_from, p_to)`, by `starts_at, id` (disjoint, thanks to `time_off_no_overlap`).
   - `O` empty → insert `(p_from, p_to, 'leave')`; `created: true`.
   - One row covering `[p_from, p_to)` → nothing written; `created: false, extended: false` (its reason stays).
   - Otherwise → keep the first row of `O`, delete the others, then set its `starts_at = least(all starts, p_from)`, `ends_at = greatest(all ends, p_to)` (reason kept); `created: false, extended: true`.
4. Never touches appointments. Calling it again changes nothing (idempotent).

Returns `{ time_off_id, staff_id, starts_at, ends_at, reason, created, extended, conflicts: [ <rows of §2.5.2 as objects, same keys> ] }`, `conflicts` = `schedule_conflicts_core(p_business_id, p_staff_id, p_from, p_to)`.

#### 2.5.4 `reassign_candidates`

1. Membership; appointment of the business (`42501`); role: owner/manager, or `appointment.staff_id = private.my_staff_id(p_business_id)` (1.4 §0.2), else `42501`; status not `booked`/`confirmed` → **AN020**.
2. For every **active** staff member `s ≠ appointment.staff_id`, by `sort, id`: terms = `private.booking_staff_terms(b, service_ids_by_position, s, false)` (AN003 caught → blocker `not_offered`); then `private.staff_time_check(b, s, a.starts_at, a.starts_at + terms.duration, terms.buffer, a.id)`: `service_overlap` → `busy`; `outside_hours` → `off` (not working then: hours, closure, time off); `buffer_overlap` → `tight`; else free.

Rows: `staff_id uuid, free boolean, blocker text` (`blocker` null iff free; ⊆ `REASSIGN_BLOCKERS` = `['not_offered','busy','off','tight']`). A free row means `staff_move_appointment(…, p_new_starts_at => a.starts_at, p_new_staff_id => s, flags false)` passes unless something changed meanwhile (then AN001/AN005/AN006/AN008, handled in §4.9).

#### 2.5.5 `save_service`

1. Membership; `p_service_id` not null (`22023` after the role check) and, if it exists, of the business (`42501`); role.
2. Shape (`22023`): `p_service` an object with **exactly** `name` (string), `category_id` (uuid string or null), `duration_min`, `buffer_after_min`, `price_cents` (integers), `online_bookable`, `active` (booleans); `p_offers` an array of ≤ 50 objects with exactly `staff_id` (uuid string), `custom_duration_min`, `custom_price_cents` (integer or null); no `staff_id` twice. `category_id` not null and not of the business, or a `staff_id` not of the business → `42501`.
3. Upsert the service by `id` (`name = btrim(name)`; `sort` = `coalesce(max(sort) + 1, 0)` of the business **only on insert**, unchanged on update); range violations surface as the table CHECKs (`23514`).
4. `delete from staff_services where business_id = b and service_id = id and staff_id <> all(<offer staff ids>)`; upsert every offer on `(business_id, staff_id, service_id)` setting both custom columns exactly as sent (null = the service default).
5. A replay with the same payload changes nothing (`created: false`).

Returns `{ id, created, name, category_id, duration_min, buffer_after_min, price_cents, online_bookable, active, sort, offers: [{ staff_id, custom_duration_min, custom_price_cents }] (by staff sort, id) }`. Existing appointments keep their copied price/duration (SPEC §7).

#### 2.5.6 `set_staff_order`

1. Membership; every id of the business (`42501`); role; `p_staff_ids` null, with a null/duplicate, or not **exactly** the set of all staff of the business (active and inactive) → `22023`.
2. Lock the business's staff rows `for no key update` in id order; `sort = ordinality − 1`.

Returns `[{ id, sort }]` in the new order. Same list again → same result.

### 2.6 Re-plan of queued reminders (D6)

`private.replan_reminders_impl(p_business_id uuid, p_now timestamptz default now()) returns integer` (SECURITY DEFINER, no grants): for every `messages_log` row `m` of the business with `template = 'reminder' and status = 'queued' and attempts = 0 and scheduled_for > p_now`, whose appointment `a` is `booked`/`confirmed` with `starts_at > p_now`: `v_at := private.reminder_at(a.starts_at, b.timezone, b.reminder_mode, b.quiet_start, b.quiet_end, m.created_at)`; `v_at is null or v_at <= p_now` → `status 'cancelled', error 'superseded', updated_at p_now`; `v_at <> m.scheduled_for` → `scheduled_for = v_at, updated_at = p_now`. Returns the number of rows changed. Due rows (`scheduled_for <= p_now`), retries (`attempts > 0`), sent/cancelled rows and other businesses are never touched.

```
create trigger businesses_replan_reminders
  after update of quiet_start, quiet_end, reminder_mode on public.businesses
  for each row
```

```ts
export function useSettingsMutation<V, R>(o: {
```

1. **Editing a time off with another staff member chosen was silently dropped** (`TimeOffSheet`; reported three times).
