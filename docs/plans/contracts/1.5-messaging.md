## 0. Decisions (defaults binding for the builders; approved by Michalis on 2026-10-01)

**Planner and time**
1. **Clock of the planner.** The signature stays `plan_messages_impl(p_appointment_id, p_change)` (plan «Κανόνες»), so it plans against the transaction's `now()`. Every time rule lives in the pure SQL function `private.reminder_at(…, p_now)` (§2.6), which pgTAP calls with fixed instants around 2026-10-25 (and 2026-03-29, America/New_York); planner integration tests use times relative to `now()`.
2. **"24 h before" = the same local wall-clock time on the previous local date** (`(starts_at at time zone tz) - interval '1 day'` on `timestamp`), not 24 elapsed hours: 25 h before the Sunday of 2026-10-25, 23 h before the Sunday of 2026-03-29. It keeps the reminder at the hour the client booked and makes quiet hours (local) behave the same all year.
3. **Late or too-early reminders are dropped, never moved later.** After the quiet-hours shift, a reminder earlier than **planning instant + 2 h** is not planned (the same 2 h margin that «≥ 26 h» gives the unshifted 24 h reminder). This covers "already in the past", "before the booking" and "minutes after the booking". SPEC says «νωρίτερα» only; a later fallback (e.g. `quiet_end`) would pass the cancellation notice.
4. **Imports:** the ≥ 26 h and + 2 h rules apply with the import instant as the planning instant (a future appointment whose reminder time already passed gets none). `import_reminders` is checked at planning **and** at claim.
5. **Source `staff`** (booked in the shop, e.g. at checkout) follows the phone rule: confirmation only if it starts in ≥ 2 h.
6. **Staff cancel with notify** uses the template of `appointments.cancelled_by`: reason `client_request` (the client phoned) → `cancelled_by_client` («ακύρωσες…»); any other reason → `cancelled_by_business`. Both carry `/r/<code>`.
7. **A staff move without notify** supersedes every queued SMS of the appointment (v1 rule, now for every actor: a still-queued confirmation included) and re-plans the reminder only.
8. **Settings changes** (quiet hours, reminder mode, `import_reminders`) apply to reminders planned afterwards; queued reminders keep their time (1.6 may add a re-plan).
9. **`messaging_enabled = false`** does not stop planning: reminders are still planned and are cancelled at claim (`messaging_disabled`), so switching back on works for bookings made meanwhile.

**Push**
10. **Recipients** (server-derived at plan time, re-checked at claim): the member(s) whose `business_members.staff_id` is the appointment's staff member (and, on a reassign, the previous one), plus every owner; managers and others only through `member_notification_prefs` (no UI yet). **The acting user is excluded** (`auth.uid()`). A push row is planned only for a recipient who has ≥ 1 `push_subscriptions` row at that moment.
11. **Events:** push for a new **online** booking, for every move (client or staff) and every cancel (client or staff) of an appointment that was active and starts in the future. No push for bookings made in the app, status changes, auto-complete or system actions (SPEC §12: «νέα online κράτηση»).
12. **Content:** event, the client's **first name only** (first word of `full_name`; «Πελάτης»/"Client" when none), first service, local date/time, staff name. Never phone, surname, notes or amounts. The payload carries el and en (OneSignal picks by device language); the row's `locale` is `businesses.locale`.
13. **Deletion of push rows on membership changes:** a trigger on `business_members` drops **all** of a user's `push_subscriptions` on **every** delete of a membership row and every change of role (not only the last business): in 1.7 each of these revokes all sessions, the devices opt out on `SIGNED_OUT`, and every device must re-enable anyway. 1.7's `private.revoke_user_sessions_impl` must also call `private.drop_push_subscriptions(user_id)` (mfa-reset, 1.9 reaction), and the 1.7 «Αποσύνδεση από όλες τις συσκευές» calls `unregister_push_subscription(p_all => true)` before `signOut({ scope: 'global' })` (closes the known limit of ADR-0010 «Συνέπειες»).
14. **`register_push_subscription` / `unregister_push_subscription` take no `p_business_id`**: subscriptions are per user (ADR-0010 §2). Second exception to «κάθε RPC παίρνει πρώτη παράμετρο `p_business_id`», after `authorize_factor_change`. `register` requires membership in at least one business; `unregister` only a session.
15. **Test push:** any member, to **all of the caller's own** subscriptions, through `request_test_push(p_business_id)` → nudge → `dispatch`; at most one per user per minute.
16. **`push_enabled`** is a new platform kill switch for push; `sms_enabled` stays SMS only.
17. `spike-push` stays in the repo, unused by the UI, until 1.10 deletes it; its template `spike_test` is renamed `push_test` (one template for both).
18. The OneSignal sender is implemented now behind the interface (Vitest with a fake `fetch`) and first used in 1.10. VAPID rows are accepted and stored, but 1.5 has no VAPID sender: VAPID targets are skipped.
19. **pg_net grants:** accepted as a platform default we cannot revoke; `net` is not an exposed PostgREST schema (`config.toml` `[api] schemas = public, graphql_public`), so no API role reaches it over HTTP. The dispatch secret sits in `net.http_request_queue` only until the worker sends it.

**Sending and brakes**
20. **Caps.** Business daily cap (count) = `least(platform.sms_per_business_day, businesses.sms_daily_cap)` over all non-OTP SMS (over → `rate_limited`, as 0005). Business **monthly budget stops reminders and marketing only** (`budget_exceeded`); confirmations, cancellations and changes (the safety net of ADR-0006) keep going, bounded by the per-phone and platform caps. New **platform monthly cap** (count, all SMS incl. OTP claims, no reserve). Monthly windows are **UTC calendar months** (like the UTC days of 1.3).
21. **Deadlines** (a queued row past its deadline is cancelled `expired` at claim): reminder → `least(scheduled_for + 60′, starts_at)` and never while the business is inside its quiet hours; push → `scheduled_for + 60′`; test push → `+ 10′`. **No deadline** for OTP and for confirmations/changes/cancellations (as 1.3): the due claim never takes OTP and the sweep closes an OTP whose challenge expired; a transactional SMS that waited through a long platform switch-off is the operator's call (cancel by hand). This also keeps `10_public_booking`, whose claim clock follows the real `now()`, independent of the calendar.
22. **Retries:** a send that certainly did not reach the provider (`failed`) is re-queued up to 3 attempts in total, after 5′ and 10′, if the deadline allows; OTP never. `unknown` (timeout, exception, lease expired) is **never** retried. A DLR `undelivered` is final.
23. **Expired lease = `unknown`**, never re-sent. The plan's «ξαναπαίρνει τα ληγμένα» is read as: the sweep takes the row back from the dead sender (its late result is refused) and closes it as `unknown`; re-sending would break the exit criterion «at most once when killed mid-batch». Cost: the unsent rest of a killed batch (≤ 5 rows) ends `unknown` (visible, not lost silently).
24. **Re-addressing at claim:** SMS rows with a `client_id` go to the client's **current** `phone_e164`/`locale` (a corrected typo wins; an erased client → `no_recipient`). SMS to a `+30` number that is not `+3069` → cancelled `not_mobile` (landlines).
25. **The sweep nudges `dispatch` every 5′ even with nothing due**, so `dispatch` writes its own heartbeat (`job_runs.job = 'dispatch'`) for 1.9's health.
26. **Purge** deletes OTP message rows together with their challenges (30 days after expiry, earlier than the 12 months of `messages_log`), nulls `messages_log.booking_token_id` of purged tokens, and trims `cron.job_run_details` to 7 days.
27. **`private.queued_sms_ids` keeps its name** (callers in 0005/0006 unchanged) but now returns the **due** queued rows of **both** channels; `private.sms_newly_queued` counts SMS rows only. So `message_ids` of `book`/`manage_*` include push rows, and `sms_queued` of the 1.4 RPCs means "an SMS to the client was queued for now".
28. `staff_book_appointment` keeps its response (no `sms_queued`): quick add says nothing about the confirmation SMS in 1.5.
29. An OTP whose send ends `unknown` counts as sent for the `start` answer (the page shows the code step; resend works after the cooldown).
30. `businesses` value rules: quiet window 1 h–12 h, whole minutes, `quiet_start ≠ quiet_end`; `sms_sender_id` 1–11 of `[A-Za-z0-9 .-]`, starting and ending alphanumeric, with at least one letter (null = platform sender, 1.10); `sms_daily_cap` null = platform default; `sms_monthly_budget_cents` null = no budget; `import_reminders` default false.

**Pro app**
31. A third bottom tab «Ρυθμίσεις» → `/settings` (index with one entry in 1.5, «Ειδοποιήσεις»; 1.6 adds its screens) → `/settings/notifications`. Every role sees it (notifications are per user).
32. `member_notification_prefs`: `null` columns = role defaults; `authenticated` may SELECT its own rows; no writes until a UI exists.

### 2.6 `private.reminder_at` — the only place of the reminder time rules

`private.reminder_at(p_starts_at timestamptz, p_timezone text, p_mode text, p_quiet_start time, p_quiet_end time, p_now timestamptz) returns timestamptz` (stable; null = no reminder):

```
loc  := p_starts_at at time zone p_timezone                       -- local wall clock (timestamp)
base := case p_mode when '24h' then loc - interval '1 day'        -- same wall-clock time, previous local date (D2)
                    when 'evening_before' then (loc::date - 1) + time '18:00' end
if base::time is inside [quiet_start, quiet_end) (crossing midnight when quiet_start > quiet_end):
   night := case when quiet_start > quiet_end and base::time < quiet_end then base::date - 1 else base::date end
   base  := night + quiet_start - interval '1 hour'                -- "earlier, quiet_start − 1 h before the night it falls in"
at := base at time zone p_timezone                                 -- PostgreSQL's rule for a gap/overlap hour
if p_starts_at - p_now < interval '26 hours' then return null      -- booked < 26 h before (SPEC §12)
if at < p_now + interval '2 hours'           then return null      -- D3
return at
```

### 2.7 Planner v2 (`create or replace private.plan_messages_impl(p_appointment_id uuid, p_change text)`, invoker, signature unchanged)

## 3. Edge Functions

### 3.1 `dispatch` (`supabase/functions/dispatch/index.ts`; logic in `_shared/dispatch-handler.ts`, runtime in `_shared/dispatch-runtime.ts`)

- `[functions.dispatch]` `verify_jwt = false`, shared import map. **Not** on the proxy allow-list (`proxy-contract.test.ts`: `POST /functions/v1/dispatch` → false). Called only by pg_net (nudge, sweep) and tests.
- Order: `POST` only (405) → configuration (invalid → 500 `not_configured`, every request) → header `x-anaklo-dispatch-secret` compared in constant time with `DISPATCH_SECRET` (missing/wrong → 403 `forbidden`; empty configured secret → 500, never accepted; same helper as `requireProxy`) → body ≤ 1 KB, `zod/mini` `strictObject({ source: enum('nudge','sweep','test') })` (400 `invalid_body`).
- Work: `started = now`; up to **5 rounds**, each `claim_due_messages(p_limit => 5)` → for every item render and send (§3.2) → `record_send_result`; stop when `more` is false, after 5 rounds or after 25 s. Then `record_dispatch_run(started, ok, sent_count, error_code)` (`ok` false when a claim RPC failed; error = SQLSTATE or code). Response 200 `{ rounds, claimed, sent, failed, rejected, unknown }` (no ids, no personal data).
- **Killed mid-batch:** claimed rows stay `sending` until `lease_until` (60 s); no claim ever returns a `sending` row; the next sweep turns them into `unknown`; a late `record_send_result` of the dead lease returns false. Each message is sent at most once; ≤ 5 rows per kill end `unknown` (D23). Provider timeout 8 s, so a batch of 5 ends well inside its lease.
- One JSON log line per event (`consoleLog('dispatch')`): ids, templates, outcome codes, counts; never phones, tokens, subscription ids, names.

**Choices the builders made where this contract was open (kept; the tests pin them)**
- DB:
  - A staff cancel with notify and reason `client_request` is keyed by its template (`appt:<id>:cancelled_by_client`), so a cancel has one notice whoever records it.
  - The due claim does not examine SMS rows while their channel is stopped (switch, the day's non-OTP room, the month's room), so push rows are never starved behind them.
  - `record_send_result` checks `lease_id` while the row is `sending`, not `lease_until`: a late result is refused once the sweep has made the row `unknown`. Since only `queued` rows are claimed, nothing else can hold it meanwhile.
  - A retry moves `scheduled_for`, so a reminder or push deadline can drift by at most 15′. It never passes the start or the quiet hours.
  - `record_dispatch_run`: `ok` false without an error stores `'failed'`.
  - DLR: `delivered` on `delivered` and `undelivered` on `failed` return false and change nothing. Only SMS rows are looked up.
  - A second test push in the same minute returns `replayed: true` and nudges again.
  - The sweep and the purge declare no actor (they write no appointment).
  - The purge counts every deleted row, including `cron.job_run_details`. `postgres` may delete there locally, so step 7 stays. Whether it may on the hosted project is checked by `db:test:dev` in 1.10.
  - `rejected`/`unknown`/`failed` keep the provider name.
- Edge Functions:
  - `dispatch` answers 413 `payload_too_large` for a body over 1 KB (the shared `parseJsonBody`), not 400.
  - A OneSignal 2xx with an unreadable body → `unknown 'invalid_response'`.
  - `dispatch-race.test.ts` puts its psql rows in the year 2000 at fixed clocks rather than `now() + 1 day`, which the live dispatcher would claim or sweep. It waits for a quiet window (40–240 s after a 5′ cron boundary, no pending nudge, no dispatch run in the last 10 s), so its timeout is 300 s.
- UI:
  - The device id is stored **before** `register`, so a sign-out still unregisters it when the register answer was lost.
  - A new subscription whose id never arrives is opted out too, and a failed opt-out is retried at the next start.
  - «Needs install» is decided when the page opens (`ProShell` shows the install screen then anyway).
  - A failed SDK load is retried by «Ενεργοποίηση».
  - `replayed` shows «Στάλθηκε».
  - «Αποσύνδεση από όλες τις συσκευές» still removes only this device's row. `unregister_push_subscription(p_all => true)` arrives with 1.7 (D13), noted in `session.ts`.

**Review fixes (2026-09-29, after the integration; each with a test that fails without it, checked by mutation)**
- **Greek landline: `sms_queued` promised an SMS the claim then cancelled (two findings, one cause). Fixed.** The planner tested only that a phone exists, and the claim cancelled `+302…` as `not_mobile`. So a staff cancel or move with notify answered `sms_queued: true`, and the sheet said «Ο πελάτης θα ενημερωθεί με SMS.». Confirmations and reminders were also queued that could never go out. The rule now lives in one SQL helper, `private.sms_reachable` (§2.5). The planner's `sms_ok` uses it, so nothing is planned for a landline and `sms_queued` is false (`notify.notSent`). Claim rule 5 uses it too, as the second guard for a number changed after planning. The pro app no longer offers the toggle for such a client: `canNotifyClient` (`appointments/rules.ts`) uses the new `canReceiveSms` of `_shared/phone.ts` (a UI hint like `isGreekMobile` on the booking page; the server still decides).
