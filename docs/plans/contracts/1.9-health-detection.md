## 0. Decisions (defaults binding for the builders; ⚠ = beyond or against the plan text)

**Health**
1. **Staleness = age of the latest `ok` run** (`job_runs.finished_at`, `ok = true`). A job that runs but fails every time is as stale as a dead one (1.4 carried).
2. **Thresholds = three missed periods:** `dispatch` 15′, `dispatch_sweep` 15′, `detect_factor_changes` 15′ (all fed every 5′), `auto_complete` 30′ (every 10′), `purge` 26 h (daily). The plan names no threshold for `dispatch_sweep`; 15′ makes «the dispatch cron stops → 503 within 15′» hold even while staff actions keep nudging `dispatch`.
3. **A job that never ran** is stale only once its threshold has passed since it began to be **watched** (`private.health_jobs.watched_since`, set by the migration). Without it a fresh `db:reset`/`db:push` would answer 503 for up to 26 h, until the first nightly purge.
4. ⚠ **Health also reports the oldest unfinished security event** (`security_events`, stale > 15′): an event detected but not contained and notified pages like a dead job.
5. **`health` stays public through the Worker** (`/api/functions/v1/health`, for the 1.10 uptime monitor, which has no credential) and refuses direct calls (proxy secret, 1.1). The body carries only check names, ages, thresholds and stale flags (and the existing `region`): never counts, ids, users or businesses. `public.health()` is `service_role` only.

**Detection**
6. **Scope = verified factors (any `factor_type`) of users who are owner or manager in some business now.** *Added* = in scope and not in the snapshot (this includes a factor verified after an earlier run, and a factor a user already had when promoted from staff: no grant can match it). *Removed* = in the snapshot and gone from `auth.mfa_factors`. In the snapshot, still existing, but out of scope → dropped silently (normally impossible: the D1 trigger deletes the factors of a user who stops being owner/manager everywhere). Unverified factors are ignored until verified.
7. ⚠ **Add matching tolerates 30 s of clock skew**: a factor matches an unmatched `add` grant of the same user when `g.created_at − 30 s ≤ f.created_at ≤ g.expires_at + 30 s` (GoTrue stamps `created_at` with its own clock, the grants use Postgres `now()`). The plan's window is `[created_at, expires_at]`. **Changed by the review fixes (§8):** `[g.created_at, g.expires_at + 30 s]`, never before the grant (`private.match_add_grants`).
8. **Remove matching:** an unmatched `remove` grant of the same user and `factor_id` with `g.expires_at > s.last_seen_at` (the factor was last seen by the run at `last_seen_at`; the grant was still valid after that). A legit removal stays matched while the detector was down; a stale grant reused long after is not.
9. ⚠ **Sessions are revoked at detection**, in the detector's transaction (containment never waits for the Edge runtime), **and again by `dispatch`** right after it deletes an added factor (closes a session opened with the attacker's factor in between). The plan names only `dispatch`.
10. **The `system` remove grant is written by `claim_security_events`** (each attempt of an *added* event whose factor still exists), committed before `dispatch` calls `auth.admin.mfa.deleteFactor`. The plan's «`write_factor_grant` or an RPC over it».

**Reaction (`dispatch`)**
11. **Containment is retried until it succeeds; notifications go at most once.** Containment (delete + revoke) is idempotent; notifications are sent only after `contained` is recorded, and a lease lost while notifying ends `notify_unknown`, never re-sent. Lease 120 s. No maximum of attempts: health pages after 15′ (D4).
12. ⚠ **The owners' push goes through `messages_log`** (new template `push_security_alert`, queued at the `contained` transition, sent by the existing push pipeline): its leases, at-most-once, `push_enabled` switch, deadline (60′) and recording are reused. **Emails go direct** (no email channel in `messages_log`: no addresses are stored).
13. **Recipients** (plan): the email of the affected account, and every **owner** (not managers) of every business where the user was owner/manager at detection (`security_events.business_ids`), except the user themself; push to the same owners with ≥ 1 device. Language and time zone: the business's (`businesses.locale`, `timezone`); the user's from the first of `business_ids` (owner memberships first, then manager, each by membership `created_at`), fallback `el`/`UTC`.
14. ⚠ **Email content:** what happened, when (business zone), what was done, what to do, the Nous address. Never a link, a code, an IP, the device's friendly name (attacker-controlled text) or a factor id. The **owner** email names the affected account by its **email address** (the owner already sees it in Ρυθμίσεις → Μέλη; without it the owner cannot act). Push: business name only. The texts of §3.4 are drafts for Michalis (prerequisite «Πριν το 1.9»).
15. **Email sender:** an interface next to `sms-provider`/`push-provider`; **only the fake sender in 1.9** (`EMAIL_PROVIDER=fake`). Resend (`resend`, `RESEND_API_KEY`, `EMAIL_FROM`) is 1.10. Missing or invalid email configuration → `dispatch` answers 500 `not_configured` for everything (fail closed, as `PUSH_PROVIDER` in 1.5).
16. **A failed email is counted and logged, never retried**; the event still ends `notified` with its counts.

**Retention, audit, tests**
17. Purge: grants 30 days after `greatest(expires_at, matched_at)`; ⚠ `done` security events 12 months after `handled_at` (as `messages_log`); unfinished events and the snapshot never.
18. Audit (actor `system`): one row per business of `business_ids` at detection; none when the user is owner/manager nowhere (the event row remains).
19. ⚠ **Automated drill** `tests/db/security-detection.test.ts` (in `test:race`): a factor added and one removed **directly at GoTrue** with an `aal2` session, the detector called through psql instead of waiting; with `SECURITY_DRILL_CRON=1` the same file waits for the real cron (the plan's manual check).
20. **Not in 1.9:** a trigger on `auth.mfa_factors` (1.10 check); a session check in `session_mfa_ok` (an issued access token keeps passing RLS ≤ 1 h after revocation: residual risk, §7 option); a Nous copy of security emails (§7 option; 1.10 Sentry alert on the `security_event` log).
21. **Estimate:** D3, D4, D9, D12 and D19 add ≈ 0.5 day to the 1.5 of 1.9 (from the buffer).

### 2.5 `private.detect_factor_changes_impl(p_now timestamptz default now()) returns integer` (volatile, definer, no grants)

Shape of `auto_complete_impl`: `set_config('anaklo.actor_type', 'system', true)` first; JWT claims hidden during the run and restored after; `started := clock_timestamp()`; the work in an inner block; `exception when others` → the inner work rolls back, `record_job_run('detect_factor_changes', started, false, null, sqlstate)`, return null; else `record_job_run(…, true, <events written>)` and return that count. `p_now` null → `22023` (recorded as a failed run).

Inside, in this order:
1. `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended('detect_factor_changes', 0))` (two runs never interleave; the second sees the first's snapshot).
2. **Current set C, read once** (one statement, kept for the whole run): `select f.id, f.user_id, f.created_at from auth.mfa_factors f where f.status = 'verified' and exists (business_members m: m.user_id = f.user_id and m.role in ('owner','manager'))`. Every later step derives from C; nothing re-reads `auth.mfa_factors` except step 4's existence test.
3. **Added** = C minus snapshot, by `factor_id`, processed in `(created_at, id)` order. For each: the earliest (`created_at, id`) unmatched `add` grant of the same user with `g.created_at − 30 s ≤ f.created_at ≤ g.expires_at + 30 s` (D7), locked `for update`; found → `matched_at := p_now`; none → event (step 5). **As fixed (§8 «Review fixes»):** window `[g.created_at, g.expires_at + 30 s]` through `private.match_add_grants`, and the factors verified and removed between two runs (GoTrue's audit log) join this pass.
4. **Removed** = snapshot rows whose `factor_id` no longer exists in `auth.mfa_factors` (any status), in `(factor_created_at, factor_id)` order: the earliest unmatched `remove` grant with the same `user_id` and `factor_id` and `g.expires_at > s.last_seen_at` (D8) → `matched_at := p_now`; none → event. Snapshot rows whose factor still exists but is not in C → dropped, no event (D6).
5. **Event:** `insert … (user_id, kind, factor_id, factor_created_at, business_ids, detected_at = p_now) on conflict (kind, factor_id) do nothing`; `business_ids` = the user's `business_members` rows with role owner/manager, ordered owner first, then manager, each by membership `created_at`, then `business_id`. For each inserted event: one `audit_log` row per element of `business_ids` (§2.8).
6. **Containment at detection (D9):** `perform private.revoke_user_sessions_impl(u)` once per distinct user with an inserted event (sessions and push devices).
7. **Snapshot := C** (as read in step 2): insert new rows (`first_seen_at = last_seen_at = p_now`), set `last_seen_at = p_now` on kept rows, delete every row not in C.
8. Any event inserted → `perform private.nudge_dispatch('security')` (sent by pg_net after commit; `dispatch` claims the event at once; the sweep's nudge every 5′ is the fallback).

Properties (pgTAP §5.1-B): a second run with no change writes no event, audit row, grant change or nudge (only `last_seen_at` and its `job_runs` row). **Concurrency with grant writers:** every writer commits its grant before (or in the same transaction as) the factor change — `authorize_factor_change` before the app's enrol/delete, `record_support_action` before `mfa-reset`'s deletes, the D1 trigger in the deleting transaction, `claim_security_events` before `dispatch` deletes — so any change visible in C is visible with its grant. A change committed after step 2 is seen by the next run.

`select cron.schedule('detect-factor-changes', '*/5 * * * *', $$select private.detect_factor_changes_impl()$$);`

### 2.6 Reaction RPCs (`service_role` only: wrapper + `_impl`) and the notification helper

**State machine** (one row per event):
```
pending ──claim (attempts+1, lease 120 s; added: system grant)──▶ containing
containing ──'contained' (current lease)──▶ notifying (lease renewed 120 s; owners' push queued; email bundle returned)
containing ──'contain_failed' (current lease)──▶ pending (lease cleared, error kept)          ← retried by the next run
containing ──lease expired──▶ claimable again (containment is idempotent)
notifying ──'notified' (current lease)──▶ done, result 'notified', counts
notifying ──lease expired (closed by the next claim)──▶ done, result 'notify_unknown'           ← never re-sent
```
A result whose `lease_id` is not the row's current one, or whose outcome does not fit the row's status, is refused (`{ "recorded": false }`, no error), like `record_send_result` (1.5).

| Wrapper | `_impl` adds | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| `claim_security_events(p_limit integer)` | `p_now timestamptz default now()` | jsonb `{ items, more }` |
| `record_security_event_result(p_id uuid, p_lease_id uuid, p_outcome text, p_emails_sent integer default null, p_emails_failed integer default null, p_error text default null)` | `p_now` | jsonb |

**`claim_security_events_impl`**: `p_now` null or `p_limit` null/not 1–10 → `22023`. (1) Close abandoned notifications: `status = 'notifying' and lease_until < p_now` → `done`, `notify_unknown`, `handled_at = p_now`, lease cleared. (2) Candidates `status = 'pending'` or (`status = 'containing'` and `lease_until < p_now`), ordered `detected_at, id`, `limit p_limit`, `for update skip locked`. (3) Each: `status 'containing'`, `attempts + 1`, new `lease_id`, `lease_until = p_now + 120 s`; *added* and the factor still exists in `auth.mfa_factors` → `private.write_factor_grant(user_id, 'remove', factor_id, 'system')` (D10). Item: `{ id, lease_id, kind, user_id, factor_id, attempts }`; `more` = (items = `p_limit`).

**`record_security_event_result_impl`**: `p_id`, `p_lease_id` or `p_outcome` null, outcome not in `('contained','contain_failed','notified')` → `22023`; `notified` needs both counts in 0–100 (else `22023`), the other outcomes take none (non-null → `22023`); `p_error` is kept only for `contain_failed`, stored as is when it matches `^[A-Za-z][A-Za-z0-9_.:-]{0,79}$`, else `'error'`. Row `for update`:
- `contained` (status `containing`): `status 'notifying'`, `contained_at = p_now`, `lease_until = p_now + 120 s`, `error = null`; `v := private.queue_security_notifications(p_id, p_now)`; `push_queued := v.push_queued`; returns `{ "recorded": true, "notify": v }`.
- `contain_failed` (status `containing`): `status 'pending'`, lease cleared, `error = p_error`; `{ "recorded": true }`.
- `notified` (status `notifying`): `status 'done'`, `result 'notified'`, `handled_at = p_now`, lease cleared, the two counts; `{ "recorded": true }`.

**`private.queue_security_notifications(p_event_id uuid, p_now timestamptz) returns jsonb`** (no grants; called only by the `_impl` above). For the event's user `u` (email `e` from `auth.users`) and `business_ids` `B[]`:
- **Push rows (D12):** for each `b` in `B` and each member `o` of `b` with role `owner`, `o <> u`, with ≥ 1 `push_subscriptions` row: insert `messages_log` (`business_id b`, `channel 'push'`, `template 'push_security_alert'`, `category 'transactional'`, `recipient_user_id o`, `locale = b.locale`, `scheduled_for p_now`, `dedupe_key 'security:<event>:<b>:<o>'`) `on conflict (dedupe_key) do nothing`. The running `dispatch` claims them in its message rounds (§3.2), at the latest the next sweep.
- **Returns** `{ kind, detected_at, push_queued, emails }`, `emails` ordered user first, then owners by business name and email:
  - `{ audience: 'user', to: e, locale, timezone, business_name: null, account_email: e }` — locale/timezone of `B[1]`, `el`/`UTC` when `B` is empty;
  - per `b` in `B`, per owner `o <> u` with an email: `{ audience: 'owner', to: o.email, locale: b.locale, timezone: b.timezone, business_name: b.name, account_email: e }`.
  - `e` null (impossible for email sign-in users) → `emails: []` (push still queued).

## 3. Edge Functions (FN builder)

### 3.1 `health` (`verify_jwt = false`, behind the `/api` proxy; logic in `_shared/health-handler.ts`, pure)

- Order: `requireProxy(req, PROXY_SECRET)` (403 / 500 as 1.1) → `GET` only (405) → no `SUPABASE_URL`/`SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` → 500 `not_configured` → `rpc('health')` with a 5 s abort signal (`HEALTH_RPC_TIMEOUT_MS`) → error, timeout, exception or a body that fails `HealthResult` (`zod/mini` strictObject: `ok`, `checks[]` of `{ name: enum(HEALTH_CHECK_NAMES), age_seconds: nullable non-negative int, max_age_seconds: int, stale: boolean }`) → **503** `{ error: { code: 'database_unavailable', … } }`.
- Answer: **200** `{ ok: true, region?, checks }` when no check is stale; **503** `{ ok: false, region?, checks }` otherwise. `Cache-Control: no-store` (the `json` helper). Nothing else in the body.
- Logs: `health_stale` with the stale names only; `health_unavailable` with a code.
- `index.ts`: reads `PROXY_SECRET`, `SUPABASE_URL`, `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY`, the region as today; a service-role client used through `.rpc()` only.
- Protection (D5): public via the Worker on purpose; direct calls refused by the proxy secret; the RPC is cheap (one index lookup per job, `job_runs_job_finished_idx`).

### 3.2 `dispatch`: the security phase

- `DISPATCH_SOURCES += 'security'` (the detector's nudge).
- Each run: **security phase first**, then the 1.5 message rounds (unchanged, still within the 25 s budget counted from the start of the run; they pick up the `push_security_alert` rows the phase queued), then `record_dispatch_run(started, ok = both phases ok, p_rows = messages sent + events notified, error = the first error code)`. The answer adds `security: { claimed, contained, notified, failed }` (counts only).
- Security phase (`_shared/security-handler.ts`, pure: `handleSecurityEvents(services, log, now)`), up to **5 events**, claimed **one at a time** (`claim_security_events(p_limit => 1)`), no new claim after **15 s** (`SECURITY_TIME_BUDGET_MS`); a claim error or an unparsable answer ends the phase (`ok = false`). Per item (`zod/mini`):
  1. **Contain:** *added* → `factors.deleteFactor(user_id, factor_id)` (`auth.admin.mfa.deleteFactor`; ok or 404 = done; anything else or a throw = `factor_delete_failed`); then, for both kinds, `rpc('revoke_user_sessions', { p_user_id })` (error or throw = `revoke_failed`). Both are attempted; any failure → `record_security_event_result(…, 'contain_failed', p_error => <first code>)`, nothing sent, next item.
  2. `record_security_event_result(…, 'contained')` → `{ recorded: false }` → log `security_lease_lost`, next item (another dispatcher has it).
  3. **Notify:** every email of `notify.emails` in parallel: template key `<factor_added|factor_removed>_<audience>`, variables §3.4 (`date` = `formatInZone(detected_at, timezone, 'dd/MM/yyyy', locale)`, `time` = `'HH:mm'`, `support` = `SUPPORT_EMAIL`), `emailProvider.send({ to, subject, text, idempotencyKey: 'security:<event id>:<index>' })`; ok → sent, anything else (render error, `failed`, `rejected`, `unknown`, throw) → failed (D16).
  4. `record_security_event_result(…, 'notified', p_emails_sent, p_emails_failed)`; `recorded: false` → log only.
- **Killed mid-way:** before `contained` → the lease expires, the event is claimed again, containment repeats harmlessly (404, a second revoke, a new `system` grant), nothing was sent. After `contained` → the next claim closes it `notify_unknown`: the emails are never sent twice; the push rows were queued in the `contained` transaction and follow the 1.5 at-most-once machine. A late result of a dead lease is refused.
- Logs: event id, kind, step, outcome codes and counts; never a user id, an email, a factor id or a text.
- `index.ts`: the same service-role client gives `rpc` and the `FactorsAdminPort` of `_shared/member-functions.ts` (`deleteFactor(userId, factorId)` → `{ ok: true } | { ok: false; status }`); the email provider from `email-config`.
